# ⚡ TRACKER - Daily Discipline & Habit OS (100% Serverless)

A zero-backend, 100% standalone habit, workout, and sleep tracking operating system. **No server, no terminal, and no laptop needed to use on iPhone!**

---

## 📱 How to Use on Your iPhone (Zero Laptop Required)

You do **NOT** need to keep your laptop open or run any server. Choose the method you prefer:

### Option 1: Free 24/7 Hosting (Recommended - 100% Free Forever)
You can host this tracker online in 1 minute using any free static host. Once hosted, you have a permanent link that works on your iPhone anytime, anywhere, with your laptop turned off!

* **GitHub Pages**:
  1. Push this folder to a GitHub repository.
  2. In your repo settings, go to **Pages** ➔ set branch to `main` ➔ Save.
  3. You get a permanent link: `https://yourusername.github.io/tracker`.
* **Vercel / Netlify**:
  - Drag and drop this `tracker` folder into [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) or [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) for an instant permanent link (e.g. `https://my-tracker.vercel.app`).

### Option 2: Fullscreen Offline App on Your iPhone
1. Open the tracker link in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (<span class="share-icon">⎋</span>).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. The Tracker icon will appear right on your iPhone home screen!
5. **It works 100% offline**: Thanks to the built-in Service Worker, it opens instantly and saves everything locally on your iPhone, even without internet or Wi-Fi!

## ✏️ Modify & Delete Features (Phone & PC)

* **Add Habits**: Tap **"➕ Add Habit"** to add as many habits as you want.
* **Modify Habit Names**: Tap any habit name to rename it.
* **Delete Habits**: Tap the red **🗑️** button next to any habit to delete it instantly.
* **📱 Today's Mobile Checklist**: 
  - A clean vertical checklist designed for one-thumb use on iPhone.
  - Big checkboxes, live completion percentage, inline name modification, and 1-tap delete buttons without having to scroll a 31-day table!
* **Exercise Logger**: Add exercises, edit weights, reps, and check sets, or delete exercises using the **✕** button.
* **Reset Month**: Reset current month data with the **🗑️ Reset** button.
* **Clear Profile**: Reset your biometric and physical target profile with **🗑️ Clear Profile**.

---

## ⚡ 1-Click Data Transfer (PC ↔ iPhone)

Want to move your existing PC data to your iPhone without any server or cable?
1. On your PC, click **"📋 Copy Data Code"** (in the Data Backup card or iPhone Access modal).
2. Send the copied text to your iPhone (via WhatsApp, Notes, AirDrop, or email).
3. On your iPhone, open the tracker and tap **"📥 Paste Data Code"**.
4. Done! All your habits, streaks, workouts, and profiles are instantly synced.
